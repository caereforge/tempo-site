*📅 July 2, 2026 · Leo from Caereforge*

I'm very happy to announce the release of Tempo v1.1.

This isn't a simple UI polish. It's a real overhaul, and a lot has changed:

- A new "log-line" timeline: easier to read, more compact, more effective.
- Tags and emojis to customize the look and feel of your timeline.
- Fully supported TLS ingestion, so your data is protected in transit too, not just at rest (that part depends on the sender, though).
- New providers and sources, each with its own dedicated score.
- A security panel to see exactly why something was rejected.
- A dedicated panel listing every event that needs attention.
- A new built-in theme, named after [Caereforge](https://caereforge.com), the philosophy behind Tempo.
- And plenty more to discover.

Tempo isn't just fun to build. It's a tool I use every day to check on my stack, and it's freed me from keeping a dozen dashboards open all day (along with the RAM they ate).

I get the notifications that matter from Home Assistant, but only the ones I've labeled for Tempo, so I know when a battery needs replacing. Uptime Kuma tells me the instant a server or a container goes down. Kopia tells me when a backup goes stale. All my custom scripts log their exit code into Tempo, so I never have to dig through their logs to check how they went.

And I can sort through all of it faster and more effectively than ever.

I'm proud of what Tempo is becoming, and I can't wait to see what we can make of it together with the community.

Thanks to everyone who's going to give it a try. I hope it becomes a daily driver for you, like it already is for me.

Leo from [Caereforge](https://caereforge.com)
